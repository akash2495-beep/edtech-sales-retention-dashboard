# EdTech Sales Funnel & Retention Dashboard (Power BI)

An end-to-end Power BI project analysing the sales funnel and customer retention of a K-12 online EdTech business (grades 8-12, India). It covers data cleaning in Power Query, a star-schema data model, DAX measures, and a 2-page interactive dashboard.

> Note: All data is synthetic, generated by `scripts/generate_data.py`. No real company, student or customer data is used. The patterns in the data are built in so the analysis workflow can be demonstrated end to end.

## Business questions answered

1. Where are the biggest drop-offs in the funnel (lead, connected, demo booked, demo attended, enrolled)?
2. Which lead sources, grades and teams convert best?
3. How do enrollments and conversion move month to month (seasonality)?
4. What share of customers renew, and which segments churn most?
5. Why do customers churn, and how do attendance and engagement relate to renewal?

## Key findings

- Funnel: 44,568 leads converted to 5,676 enrollments (about 12.7%). Roughly 82% of leads were connected, 45% booked a demo, and 32% attended one.
- Channels: Referral converts best (about 18%) and Meta Ads converts worst (about 10%), so channel mix and budget are worth revisiting.
- Seasonality: Enrollments peak in March to May and October, and dip in December.
- Retention: The overall renewal rate is about 40%. Grade 12 is the weakest segment at about 19%. Renewal rate improved from about 30% (2024 due dates) to about 44% (2026).
- Engagement: Renewal rate rises sharply with attendance, from about 20% below 50% attendance to about 60% at 80% and above.
- Churn reasons: Low engagement and price / affordability are the top reasons for churn.

## Data model

Star schema with 5 fact tables and 5 dimension tables (16 relationships, all many-to-one, single direction).

| Type | Tables |
|---|---|
| Facts | `fact_leads`, `fact_enrollments`, `fact_renewals`, `fact_targets`, `fact_marketing_spend` |
| Dimensions | `dim_date` (with fiscal year, Apr-Mar), `dim_mentor`, `dim_team`, `dim_lead_source`, `dim_grade` |

Renewals are analysed by renewal due date cohort, and renewals that are not yet due are excluded from the renewal rate.

## Data preparation (Power Query)

- Removed duplicate leads (60 duplicate rows)
- Trimmed and standardised `lead_source` text (case and extra spaces)
- Standardised city names (Bangalore to Bengaluru) and replaced blank cities with "Unknown"
- Converted date columns to proper date types and promoted headers
- Built a shared `dim_grade` table so the grade slicer filters leads, enrollments and renewals

## DAX measures (19)

Funnel: `Total Leads`, `Connected Leads`, `Demos Booked`, `Demos Attended`, `Enrollments`, `Lead to Enrollment %`, `Connect Rate`, `Demo Booking Rate`, `Show-up Rate`, `Demo to Enrollment %`, `Revenue (INR)`

Retention: `Renewals Due`, `Renewed`, `Churned`, `Renewal Rate`, `Churn Rate`, `Renewal Revenue (INR)`, `Avg Attendance`, `Avg Test Score`

Example:

```DAX
Renewals Due = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS(fact_renewals), fact_renewals[renewal_status] <> "Not Due Yet")
Renewal Rate = DIVIDE([Renewed], [Renewals Due])
Lead to Enrollment % = DIVIDE([Enrollments], [Total Leads])
```

## Recommendations (illustrative)

- Shift more acquisition budget towards Referral and Webinar sources, and review Meta Ads targeting.
- Add early intervention for students with attendance below 50%, since they renew at far lower rates.
- Design a dedicated retention programme for Grade 12 customers.
